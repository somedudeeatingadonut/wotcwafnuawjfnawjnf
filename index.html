About the Engine

AI World Maker is an AI-driven text adventure engine. It uses advanced language models to act as a dynamic Game Master, generating stories, consequences, and descriptions based on your unique prompts.

Please understand that this is not a traditional 3D game engine. There are no actual physical "worlds" to explore in a literal sense. Instead, the engine synthesizes AI-generated snapshots to visualize your journey.

Every scene is a hallucination of the AI, designed to provide atmosphere to the text-based core of the simulation. Your choices drive the narrative, while the visuals act as a window into the AI's interpretation of your world.

TECH NOTE: The .aiworld export files are standard ZIP archives containing your world configuration, metadata, and adventure logs.
