          How this works

          A simulation of a CS2 skin site, built so you can see the machinery instead of guessing at it.

          💸 There is no real money here

          • No deposits, no withdrawals, no payments, no crypto, no card forms, no accounts. There is no code for any of it.
          • Gems are free forever: 2,500 to start, 2,500–9,000 every 3 minutes, three free case vouchers every 45 minutes, level-ups pay bonuses.
          • Items carry simulated gem values on real CS2 item names. Nothing can be traded, transferred or withdrawn.
          • The "Case Creation" card and the payment-method buttons are deliberately dead ends — that is where the real thing takes your money.

          🤖 The other players are bots

          • 30 scripted bots with levels, balances, betting styles and chat lines. They bet, open cases, battle, complain and rebuy instantly when they bust.
          • They carry a BOT badge in chat, in bet lists, in the battle feed and on the leaderboard. Manufactured activity is the oldest trick in this genre; here it is labelled.
          • Your chat gets canned replies after a random delay. They do not understand you and they never will.

          📉 The maths, printed

          • Cases use the published CS2 rarity odds: Mil-Spec 79.92%, Restricted 15.98%, Classified 3.2%, Covert 0.64%, Rare Special 0.26%.
          • Every case prices its contents to return about 90%, which is why the median pull is a fraction of the entry.
          • Crash has 1% instant-bust rounds and ~50% of rounds die before 2.00x. Double is ≈11% a spin. Mines rakes 3%, Tiles ≈4%, Upgrader 4%, Champion 5%, Plinko 1%.
          • Small edges plus thousands of spins equals a hole. That is not bad luck, it is arithmetic.
